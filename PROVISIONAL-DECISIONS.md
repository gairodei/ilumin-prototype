# Provisional Decisions Register: client clarifications R1–R5 (Oct 2026)

These are decisions made only so the offline prototype can be used interactively. None of them is a confirmed client rule. Each entry gives the current behaviour, the reason for it, where it lives in the code, and what to change once the client confirms. Unresolved client questions are listed at the end.

Status values:
- **Provisional:** built and working, awaiting confirmation.
- **Open:** nothing has been built, because building it would mean inventing a rule.

## E1: NVQ programme duration and payment configuration (post-R1–R5 enhancement, Oct 2026)

### Confirmed requirement (user confirmation, 20 Oct 2026)

| ID | Confirmed rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CR-E1-01 | **Finance Admin approval is required for payment terms proposed by an Academic Admin or System Admin when creating a new NVQ programme.** A Draft programme can't be published, and so can't be purchased, until a Finance Admin approves its payment terms. | `e1SubmitNow`, `e1Decide`, `ch8Check` wrapper in `js/setup-e1.js`; `js/e1-apply.js` |

This rule keeps the existing governance constraints:
- Finance Admins approve the **financial terms only**. They don't approve academic content and they don't publish; publishing stays with the Super Admin, System Admin and Academic Admin.
- The Super Administrator's **D8** immediate-action authority is unchanged: their own terms apply at once.
- **Nobody approves an item they submitted** (G4 guard).
- **Only an authorized, Active Finance Admin** can approve or request changes, **before and after 7 days**. Academic Admins, System Admins, the Super Administrator and inactive Finance Admins are refused, and the refusal is logged.
- **The 7-day escalation doesn't apply to these items.** The client hasn't confirmed that escalation should move approval authority, so the Super Administrator gains none, and the Finance Admin keeps it when the item is overdue. After 7 days the item is only marked "Overdue", for information; ownership and permissions don't change. Nothing is approved or rejected automatically. Other item types escalate exactly as before.
- The Super Administrator's **own** payment-plan actions still apply immediately (D8).
- **Requesting changes needs a reason.** Approving needs a note for the record. Both checks are made in the decision path, not only in the dialog.
- **Pending terms can't become newly purchasable.**
- **Existing approved plans and historical enrolment prices don't change.**

The rule covers **new programmes only**. It does **not** extend to changes to a published programme, legacy drafts, or other workflows (PD-E1-07, PD-E1-11). The rest of E1 below remains provisional.

The design fits these final decisions:
- G5.2: only Finance Admins and the Super Administrator own payment plans.
- D8: the Super Administrator's own actions apply immediately.
- G4: nobody approves their own item, and the confirmed routing table is unchanged.
